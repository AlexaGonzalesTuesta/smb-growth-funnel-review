# Weekly business review: new-seller growth

Generated by `python -m growth.build` from the real Olist data. Lead cohort: 2018-01-01 to 2018-04-30. Seller book as of 2018-06-30.

## Headline

| Driver | Value |
|---|---|
| Leads | 4,695 |
| Win rate | 12.0% |
| Activation rate (first sale within 90 days) | 42.7% |
| GMV per activated seller (mean, 90 days) | R$1,251 |
| New-seller GMV | R$300K |

## Findings and actions

1. **57% of signed sellers never made a sale.** 322 of 562 sellers signed from this lead cohort had no sale within 90 days. Raising activation to the paid-search rate (55%) adds 30% GMV; buying 20% more leads adds 20% and costs acquisition spend.
   Action: Report activation rate next to deals closed for every rep, every week.

2. **Day 30 is the moment to step in.** Activated sellers made their first sale after a median of 39 days. Of the 554 signed sellers with no sale by day 30, only 32% sold by day 90.
   Action: Call every new seller with no sale by day 21, before the day-30 drop-off.

3. **Offline stores and manufacturers need a different onboarding track.** Offline stores activated 24% of the time and manufacturers 32%, against 49% for resellers and 63% for large online sellers.
   Action: Give offline and manufacturer sellers hands-on catalog setup in their first two weeks.

4. **Social leads close at half the average rate.** Social leads won 5.9% of the time versus 12.0% overall, and returned R$14 of GMV per lead versus R$70 for paid search. 15% of leads have no tracked source.
   Action: Tighten social lead qualification before adding budget, and fix source tracking on lead forms.

5. **One seller produced a quarter of new-seller GMV.** The largest new seller sold 25% of the cohort's 90-day GMV and the top 10% sold 67%. The median activated seller sold R$319.
   Action: Judge channels and reps on win and activation rates, not GMV per lead.

6. **Rep differences are within normal variation.** Rep activation ranged from 29% to 59%, but no rep's 90% interval excludes the team rate.
   Action: Coach the onboarding process for everyone; recheck reps when another quarter of deals closes.

7. **183 still-active sellers flagged before they stopped selling.** On June 30, 2018 the model flagged 183 of 915 sellers who had sold in the last 30 days. 25% of them made no sale in the next 60 days, versus 8% of the rest (2.2x). They sold R$157K in the 16 weeks before.
   Action: Send the at-risk list to account managers every Monday, worst first.
